Welcome to Maestria

Two more steps and your Maestros show up here.

  1. Create your account Password and two-factor are set.
  2. Link a machine Workstation is online · Link another
  3. Add a workspace In Maestri, open a workspace and run this in its folder. It installs the Maestria skill, so the workspace's Maestro can post tasks and chat here.
    maestria init

What leaves your machine

To show them here, the connector sends chats, project docs, agent briefs and the last lines of agent terminals. They travel encrypted and are stored encrypted at rest. Code, secrets and Maestri tokens never leave the machine. Prefer to keep everything local? Run maestria serve instead.
