Maestri Wire is off on Workstation

The connector is online, but it can't see agents, floors or chat without Wire. Tasks the Maestro posts still arrive.

Turn on Maestri Wire

In Maestri, open Settings → Wire and turn it on, then pair it with the connector on that machine:

maestria wire pair

The connector picks it up by itself; this page updates when it does.
